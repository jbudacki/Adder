# Build, integrate, and buy recommendation

## Recommendation

Use a hybrid strategy: own the ADDER assurance model and integration layer, integrate approved engineering scanners, and evaluate commercial services only for evidence sources or operating capabilities that are expensive to reproduce credibly.

No vendor purchase or mandatory rollout is recommended from the current pilot evidence.

## Build and own in ADDER

- Risk triage, review routing, evidence contracts, release criteria, and human decision boundaries.
- Repository-local skills and deterministic validation for stable checks.
- Finding normalization, advisory dispositions, remediation evidence, and the governed intelligence loop.
- Organization-specific policy mappings, exceptions, compatibility statements, and release records.

These elements encode the organization's risk appetite and must remain inspectable, versioned, and portable.

## Integrate existing approved capabilities

- SAST, secret scanning, software-composition analysis, IaC scanning, and CI policy engines already operated by engineering or security.
- Source-control protections, code-owner approvals, signed commits or artifacts, and deployment controls.
- Existing ticketing, evidence retention, metrics, and exception workflows.

ADDER should normalize and interpret these results rather than replace mature deterministic controls.

## Consider buying or externally sourcing

Time-boxed evaluations may be justified for:

- Authoritative package vulnerability, malware-reputation, registry-integrity, and maintainer-risk intelligence.
- AI-assistant provenance or development telemetry that cannot be obtained reliably from repository evidence.
- Enterprise reporting, support, policy distribution, signed updates, and fleet-wide compatibility management.

Any evaluation must measure incremental detection, false positives, data egress, integration cost, support quality, lock-in, and evidence exportability. Marketing claims are not substitutes for repository-level validation.

## Decision gate

Reconsider a purchase only after the representative pilot identifies a measurable gap that cannot be met safely and economically by existing approved tools or the ADDER-owned layer. Until then, continue building the portable core and integrating existing controls in advisory mode.
